## 🌠 Vision & Philosophy

Every great walking game is secretly a meditation on freedom. *Big Walk* understands this — it hands you a horizon and whispers, "go." But horizons have fences, and meditations have interruptions. **Phantom Stride** was born from a simple, stubborn question: *what if the walk never had to end at the world's edge?*

We built this suite around three principles that separate it from the noisy crowd of "trainer" utilities:

1. **Preserve the poetry.** The world's art direction, lighting, and soundscape should never be broken — only stretched. Nothing here scribbles over the canvas.
2. **Respect the rig.** A companion tool should feel lighter than a feather. Phantom Stride lives in a slim overlay that sips memory and never argues with your frame pacing.
3. **Speak every traveler's tongue.** A wanderer in Tokyo and a wanderer in Lisbon should both feel at home in the menu.

This is not a shortcut. It is a telescope.

## 🔭 What Phantom Stride Actually Does

At its core, the suite injects a real-time control layer between you and the game's simulation. That layer is invisible until summoned, and when summoned it presents a luminous radial interface — reminiscent of a compass rose — from which every function radiates outward.

The overlay hooks gently into process memory read/write pathways, applying value adjustments through a signature-matching engine that adapts to each game build. Because the signatures are re-scanned on every launch, the suite survives minor patches without drama.

## 🏃 The Velocity Weaver

Speed is not a number — it is a mood. At 0.25×, the world becomes a slow-motion ballet, raindrops hanging like glass beads in the air. At 20×, you are a rumor, a blur between two points, a thought crossing a valley before the echo of its own origin fades.

The Velocity Weaver offers **eleven presets** and one continuum slider, mapped to hotkeys you define yourself. A subtle on-screen shimmer — never obtrusive — tells you the current multiplier at a glance. The value is clamped against game-breaking extremes so that physics stays coherent even at the edges.

**Why travelers love it:**
- Traverse a slow-trodden forest in seconds when you have seen it before.
- Slow the world to appreciate a sunset you would otherwise miss.
- Sync movement to music for machinima and montage projects.

## 👻 The Ethereal Companion (Ghost Mode)

Ghost Mode is the heart of the suite and the reason many explorers arrive. When engaged, your character's collision volume is decoupled from the environment. Walls become suggestions. Cliffs become doorways. The map's carefully drawn boundary fades into a thin veil you can simply step past.

The mode ships in **three flavors**:

- **Whisper** — You keep collision with the ground but ignore walls.
- **Drift** — Full freedom in all three axes, with soft damping so you do not fly off into the void unintentionally.
- **Anchor** — Ghost through geometry but snap back to the nearest walkable surface when you release the key.

An on-screen ember indicator glows faintly when Ghost Mode is active, so there is never any confusion about your current state.

## ❓ Frequently Curious Questions

**Is this safe for my save files?**
Yes. Phantom Stride operates on live session memory and never writes to save archives. Your progress remains untouched.

**Will this work after a game update?**
Usually within hours. The signature engine re-scans on each launch, and major patches receive dedicated compatibility passes.

**Does it conflict with other overlays?**
Rarely. The compositing layer is designed to coexist with common performance monitors. If you encounter a clash, report it and we will investigate.

**Can I remap every hotkey?**
Every single one. Nothing is locked.

**Is the source readable?**
The core overlay logic is documented extensively, and the plugin layer is open for community extension.
